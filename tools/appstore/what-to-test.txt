What to test in this build:

New — coach method in plain words, hand review on iPhone (PR #307)
• Poker Coach → SEE KEY DECISION. Method is everyday language: thousands of pretend hands, every leftover card equally likely, or every hand the other player could still hold. No “Monte Carlo.”
• The note under the review says every possible hand counts, even ones a careful player would fold, and a tie is included because the pot would be split.
• iPhone, held sideways: HAND REVIEW (why the hand was won or lost, and one thing to try) is on screen when the coach opens. Swipe the card for the cards and SEE KEY DECISION.

New — two women at every table (PR #308)
• Saloon: two Cowboy Girls stay seated the whole session, each with a different skin tone. Sheriff and Bandit are still there. Table View should show both women.
• Octagon: two different female fighters stay seated the whole session. You stay a man. In Settings, switch between Saloon and Octagon; both rooms keep two women.
• Reload a table saved from an older build that only had one woman. It should now have two.

New — next song (PR #306)
• With music on, NEXT SONG sits in the top bar beside RULES. Tap it and the current song fades into the next one.
• Settings → SOUND → turn MUSIC off. NEXT SONG disappears. Turn MUSIC back on and the button returns.

New — key decision beside the coach (PR #305)
• Open Poker Coach. SEE KEY DECISION and HIGHLIGHT ON/OFF sit on one row under the cards.
• Tap SEE KEY DECISION: the write-up opens in its own window immediately to the right of the coach, and the coach shifts left so the character stays in frame. Tap again and that window closes.
• HIGHLIGHT ON fills gold and rings the winning cards. HIGHLIGHT OFF stays an outline on the same row.

New — showdown hand list (PR #303)
• Three-player showdown: the hand-compare overlay shows all three players on one row (you plus two NPCs), with cards and hand names under each seat — not two on top and one wrapped below.
• Four or more players at showdown: first row holds three seats; any extra seats wrap to a second row.
• Portrait and landscape on iPhone: long outlaw names should still fit without breaking the single-row layout for three-way pots.

New — fold-win highlights (PR #304)
• Win because everyone else folded with a full board in Poker Coach. HIGHLIGHT ON must ring all five of your best hand (board + holes), not only your two hole cards. Preflop steals still highlight holes only.

New — coach board on uncontested wins (PR #302)
• Go all-in and win when everyone folds (or take any pot with no showdown). Open Poker Coach: felt and card dock show all five community cards plus your hole cards, not only your two cards.
• Fold-win dock still shows all five community cards plus your holes when the runout is known.
