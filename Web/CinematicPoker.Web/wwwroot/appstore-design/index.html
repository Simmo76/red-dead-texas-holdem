DEAD MAN'S HAND • TEXAS HOLD'EM POKER

Apple App Store Listing Design

Positioning, store metadata and a produced screenshot set — captured from the real game in the Saloon and the Octagon, led by the Poker Coach and the learning features.

1.Positioning

One-line pitch: the poker game that teaches you poker — cinematic Texas Hold'em against five characterful AI outlaws, with a coach in your corner, entirely offline.

LEARN AS YOU PLAY

A tap-to-ask “What would AI do?” advisor on your turn (action, equity, pot odds, a plain-English reason) and a post-hand Poker Coach — Maximilian in the Saloon, Tommy Gunn in the Octagon — who reviews why you won or lost.

EXTRAORDINARY PLACES

Cinematic 3D tables with seated presence: the old-west Saloon and the cage-fighting Octagon, each with its own cast, coach, audio and atmosphere. More environments ship as one-time packs.

HONEST OFFLINE POKER

Single player, no account, no server, no real-money wagering, chips never for sale. Works on a plane. “Data Not Collected” privacy label.

3.Description

~2,340 of 4,000 characters — room left for localisation growth and a future environment list.

The poker game that teaches you poker. Pull up a chair in a smoky frontier saloon — or at a felt table pitched in the middle of a fight cage — and play Texas Hold'em against five outlaws with real personalities. No accounts. No ads between hands. No real-money bets. Just you, the cards, and a coach in your corner. A COACH IN YOUR CORNER After any hand, ask the table coach for a review. Maximilian — the eye-patch bandit holding court at the saloon bar — walks you through why you won or lost, shows the key decision, and leaves you with one thing to try next time. Play the Octagon and Tommy Gunn does the same from inside the cage. Set the coach to review every hand, only notable hands, or stay quiet until you ask. STUCK? ASK “WHAT WOULD AI DO?” On your turn, one tap reveals what a card-counting pro would do with your exact cards: the recommended action, your estimated equity, the pot odds, and a plain-English reason. Use it as training wheels, check it after you've decided, or leave it alone and play your own game. LEARN FROM SCRATCH Never played Hold'em? The rules and the full hand-rankings chart are one tap away, even mid-hand. Start by tapping “What would AI do?” every turn; within a session you'll be reading the board on your own. FIVE RIVALS WITH REAL PERSONALITIES Nickey 2-guns, Tommy Gunn, Stevie Wanders, Jimmy two-hands and Simmo Says play like people, not slot machines: some tight, some reckless, some on tilt after a bad beat. They bluff, they have tells — and sometimes the tells are lies. The AI makes decisions with genuine card mathematics plus human-like noise. It never sees your cards. EXTRAORDINARY PLACES Every table is a seat in a living 3D scene — chips riffle, cards slide, characters watch you across the felt. • THE SALOON — brass taps, bottle-lined shelves, frontier hospitality. • THE OCTAGON — heavyweight bruisers playing cards where they usually throw hands. More environments are on the way as one-time packs: new table, new cast, new coach. HONEST OFFLINE POKER • Single player, offline-first: plays on a plane, in a tunnel, anywhere. • No account, no sign-in, nothing to subscribe to. • Chips are play money and always will be. We sell places, never chips. • Real poker rules: blinds, side pots, split pots, min-raise tracking, heads-up play — the engine is validated by a 10,000-hand simulation every release. Shuffle up. The table's waiting, and the coach is buying.

7.App icon direction

The dead man's hand itself — aces and eights, fanned, on dark wood with one gold-rim poker chip. No text (the name renders below the icon anyway); two big card pips and one warm light keep it readable at 60×60. Avoid character faces, felt-green backgrounds and the word “poker” in the icon. The splash art's foreground cards/chips crop is the exact source material.
