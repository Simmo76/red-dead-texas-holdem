Dead Man's Hand

Privacy Policy & Support — last updated 17 November 2026

The short version

This game collects no data. None. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server. The entire game — the poker engine, the AI opponents, the coach — runs on your device and works with no internet connection at all.

What the app stores

Your chip stack, settings and hand history are saved only on your own device so the game can resume where you left off. This data never leaves your device, is not visible to us or to anyone else, and is deleted when you delete the app.

What the app sends over the network

Nothing. The iOS app bundles every asset it uses and makes no network requests. It is fully playable in Airplane Mode.

Real money and gambling

Dead Man's Hand is a single-player simulated poker game played against computer opponents. Chips are play money only: there is no real-money wagering, no cash-out, no prizes, and chips are never for sale.

Children

The game contains simulated gambling and carries the corresponding App Store age rating. It is not directed at children, and since it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

The browser version

If you play the web version in a browser instead of the app, the pages and game files are served by our static-site host, which may record standard web-server access logs (such as your IP address) to operate the service, as every website's host does. The game itself still collects and transmits nothing.

Support

For help, bug reports or suggestions, please open an issue on the project's issue tracker. We read everything.

Changes to this policy

If a future update ever changes any of the above (for example, optional analytics or in-app purchases for new environments), this page will be updated before that version ships, and the App Store privacy label will be updated with it.
